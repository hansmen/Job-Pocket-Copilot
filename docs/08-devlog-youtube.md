# Devlog for YouTube

A public story of the project helps both motivation and the job search. Keep the format simple: 5-12 minutes, one episode per stage or important day.

## Before publishing the repository or any video

- [ ] The git history contains no keys, tokens, `.env`, service account JSON or `google-services.json`.
- [ ] The screen shows no real postings with personal data, recruiter messages, email or phone number.
- [ ] The golden set contains only synthetic or anonymized text.
- [ ] Do not show your inbox or Apple/Google consoles with payment details.
- [ ] If you mention an AI provider's terms or prices, verify they are current.

## Episode structure

1. **Hook (up to 15 s):** the result of the episode ("today I shared a posting from Safari and it appeared in the app").
2. **Context (30 s):** which day we are on, what happened before.
3. **Work (the main part):** 2-3 moments that were interesting or hard. Show mistakes and how you solved them.
4. **Result:** what works now (a demo on the phone).
5. **Next:** what the next episode covers.

## Daily note template (2 minutes)

```
Day N: <goal>
Done: ...
Did not work / surprising: ...
Decision (+why): ...
Tomorrow: ...
```

## Stage retrospective template

- What from the plan is done, what is not and why.
- The stage metrics (from the planning document): met or not.
- The 3 biggest lessons.
- What I change in the next stage.
- What I drop from the backlog.

## Filming tips

- Screen recording + microphone; the first episode can be unedited.
- English captions if you also target an English-speaking audience (it doubles as language practice).
- Video description: a link to the repository and the planning document.
- Do not wait for perfect: episode 1 should be out by Day 4.
