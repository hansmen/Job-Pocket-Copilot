# User research

Goal: in 1-2 weeks find out whether the pain is real and collect the first 50 waitlist emails. It also trains a skill developers usually lack.

## Whom to ask

- 10 developers who are job hunting now or found a job in the last 6 months.
- Where to find them: DOU, Djinni, LinkedIn (direct messages to people you know), Telegram chats, former colleagues, Discord communities.

## Message template

> Hi! I am building a tool for people searching for IT jobs and want to understand how it really goes.
> Could we talk for 15 minutes about how you track applications? I am not selling anything.

## Interview script (15 min)

1. How many places did you apply to in the last 2 months? How do you track them (spreadsheet, notes, memory)?
2. Show me what that looks like now (screen share is fine, without personal data).
3. Recall the last time you forgot a follow-up or lost track of a posting. What happened?
4. What is hardest: tailoring the resume, preparing for interviews, English, waiting for replies?
5. Which existing tools did you use (Teal, Huntr, Notion, Excel)? What did you not like?
6. Would you pay for this? How much and for what? (Do not suggest a number.)
7. What would you want the tool to do for you?

Rules: listen more than you talk; do not pitch a solution; write down quotes verbatim; ask about past behavior, not hypothetical intentions.

## What to record

A table: name/handle, role, number of applications, tool used, main pain, quote, willingness to pay.
After 10 conversations pick the 3 recurring themes: they matter more than your assumptions.

## Waitlist page

Minimum: a headline ("All your applications and follow-ups in one place"), 3 benefits, an email field, a button.
Tool: Tally / Carrd / Framer. Do not write your own backend at this stage.

## Decisions based on results

| Signal                                                 | What we do                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pain confirmed in 6+ of 10 and some willingness to pay | proceed to Stage 2 as planned                                    |
| Pain exists but elsewhere (e.g. resumes or interviews) | change the Stage 2 priority                                      |
| Pain is weak                                           | keep it as a personal tool and learning project; do not scale it |
